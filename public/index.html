Mission statement

Empower the youth towards digital transformation technology.

Our objectives

  • Foster cross-disciplinary collaboration through mixed-team hackathons and group projects.
  • Accelerate career readiness by connecting students with industry through alumni panels and company site visits.
  • Cultivate a supportive community through peer mentorship and regular social interactions.
